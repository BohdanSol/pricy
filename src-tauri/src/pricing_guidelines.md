# Tour and transfer pricing guidelines

Follow these rules for every price you look up.

## 1. Service components

Identify which components the request needs: transportation, guide, admission tickets, trains, food tastings.

**Transportation only**
- Daily transportation: up to 10 hours.
  - Italy: prices vary with the stops and the day's itinerary.
- One-way transportation: from one location to another.
- Transportation between locations with one or more stops (up to 14 hours): use the closest matching offer.
  - Japan: for one stop, use the closest available price. If the service exceeds 5 hours, charge the full-day rate.

## 2. Pricing rules

- Always give the **net price**.
- Always write "**Net Price**" next to every price.

## 3. When there is no exact answer

Give the closest available option and say clearly that it is the closest match you could find. For example:
- a nearby city;
- the same geographical area;
- a price for a similar number of service hours.

## 4. Where to look, per service

**Transportation (not trains)**
- First search Google Drive, Freshdesk and WhatsApp.
- For services booked before through TINT, check past bookings and their prices in TINT.

**Trains**
- Search the requested route on the train websites listed in the reference links (if provided), otherwise on the official operator sites.
- Always include a booking link.

**Admission tickets**
- Use the reference links and resources first; if they don't have it, search other public websites.
- Always include a purchase link.

**Guided tours without transportation**
- The price includes the guide's fee.
- Check whether the itinerary needs admission tickets. If it does:
  - give the ticket price and a link to the official purchase site;
  - calculate the total admission cost for all participants **including the guide**;
  - show admission costs separately from the guide's daily fee.
- Guiding duration:
  - Japan: half day = up to 4.5 hours; full day = up to 10 hours.
  - Italy: hourly, half-day or full-day pricing; use the itinerary to decide the duration.

**Tours that need a vehicle**
- The total includes vehicle, guide and admission tickets (if required).

**Food tours**
- Include the cost of the food tastings. If you don't know it, say clearly that the tasting costs still need to be added.

## 5. Seasonality and dates

- Look for prices for the requested service date: ideally the same season, otherwise the closest date available.
- If there is no seasonal pricing, give the general price you have.
- Pay special attention to high season:
  - Japan and South Korea: March–May and October–November.
  - Italy: April–October.

## 6. Missing information or components

If any component is missing, or you think another component is needed, flag it in **bold**.

## 7. Several prices

If you find more than one price, present every option (they all go to Operations for approval). For each option give:
- the company (supplier) name;
- the price (Net Price).

Then ask which price should be communicated to the Sales team.
